# Shipping Policy

## Order processing
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed after 1:00 PM EST, or on weekends and holidays, start processing on the next business day. We do not ship on weekends.

## US shipping options and rates
- Standard: 5-7 business days, $5.99. Free on orders of $75 or more (before tax).
- Express: 2-3 business days, $14.99.
- Overnight: 1 business day, $24.99. Order by 1:00 PM EST. Not available to PO boxes, Alaska, or Hawaii.

Delivery times are counted from the ship date, not the order date.

## International shipping
- Canada: 7-12 business days, $15.99.
- UK and EU: 10-15 business days, $19.99.

We do not currently ship to other countries. Express and Overnight are not available for international orders. Customs duties, taxes, and import fees are the customer's responsibility and are collected by the carrier at delivery. International returns follow the same 30-day policy, but we cannot provide prepaid labels outside the US, so return postage is paid by you and reimbursed only for defective, damaged, or wrong items.

## Tracking
You will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number within 24 hours of your order shipping. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first update.

## Changing your address
The shipping address can be changed only while your order status is Processing. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed. Contact support@demokicks.example as quickly as possible if you spot a mistake.

## Delays
Weather and carrier delays are outside our control. If a Standard order has not arrived 10 business days after its ship date, contact support and we will open a carrier trace. If the package is confirmed lost, we send a replacement or issue a full refund.

## Damaged or missing packages
If your order arrives damaged, email photos within 7 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or refund at no cost. If tracking says delivered but you cannot find the package, wait 24 hours, check with neighbors, and then contact us within 7 days of the delivery scan.

## Shipping restrictions
Express and Overnight orders cannot be delivered to PO boxes. Standard shipping to PO boxes is available within the US.
