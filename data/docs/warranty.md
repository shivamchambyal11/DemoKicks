# Warranty Policy

## Coverage period
All DemoKicks shoes are covered against manufacturing defects for 12 months from the delivery date. The warranty applies to the original purchaser only and is not transferable.

## What is covered
- Sole separation or delamination at the factory bond
- Stitching that comes apart under normal use
- Defective eyelets, zippers, or straps
- Peeling or cracking of lining or upper material that is not caused by wear

Final Sale items are also covered for manufacturing defects.

## What is not covered
- Normal wear and tear, including worn outsole tread and softened cushioning
- Damage from washing machines, dryers, heaters, or other direct heat
- Stains, odor, discoloration, or color fading
- Damage from accidents, cuts, burns, or pets
- Shoes altered or repaired by a third party
- Wrong size or fit issues (use the Returns Policy within the return window)

## How to make a claim
Email support@demokicks.example with your order number, clear photos of the defect (a close-up and a photo of the whole shoe), and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 business days. You do not need to send the shoes back before your claim is approved.

## Resolution
If your claim is approved, we choose one of the following: a replacement in the same style and size (if available), a refund to your original payment method, or store credit if the style has been discontinued. Shipping is free for approved claims, including a prepaid label if we ask for the pair to be sent back for inspection. Refunds are issued 5-7 business days after we receive the shoes.

## Warranty versus returns
During the 30-day return window (or the holiday extension), a defective pair can go through either route. A return is usually faster if you want a refund. After the return window ends, the warranty is your only option, and it does not cover fit or comfort complaints.

## Care tips
To keep your shoes in good shape, clean them by hand with a soft brush, mild soap, and cold water. Air dry them away from heat and rotate between pairs. Machine washing is not recommended, and damage caused by it is not covered.
