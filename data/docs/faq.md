# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

### How do I check my order status?
Give this chatbot your order number (format DK1234) and the email used at checkout, or log in to your account and open Orders. Statuses are Processing, Shipped, Delivered, and Cancelled.

### Can I cancel my order?
Yes, while the status is Processing. Contact support@demokicks.example with your order number. Once the status is Shipped, the order cannot be cancelled. Wait for delivery and start a return instead. Cancellation refunds go to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

### Can I change the size or style after ordering?
Only while the order is Processing: cancel and place a new order. After delivery, use a free exchange (see the Returns Policy).

## Payments

### Which payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not offer cash on delivery. Your payment is charged when the order is placed.

### How do promo codes work?
One code can be used per order and codes cannot be combined. Codes do not apply to gift cards or Final Sale items. A code must be entered at checkout and cannot be added after the order is placed.

### Do you offer price adjustments?
If the price of an item you bought drops within 14 days of your purchase, contact support and we will refund the difference. Final Sale items are not eligible.

## Products

### What if my size is sold out?
Use the Notify Me button on the product page and we will email you when it is restocked. Restocks are not guaranteed.

### Do you sell gift cards?
Yes, digital gift cards from $25 to $250. They are emailed within 1 hour, never expire, and are non-refundable.

## Contact and support

### How do I reach a human?
Email support@demokicks.example (replies within 1 business day) or use live chat with a human agent Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. This chatbot is available 24/7 and will pass you to a human if it cannot answer.

### Do you have a physical store?
No, DemoKicks is online only.

### I forgot my password. What do I do?
Click Forgot password on the login page. The reset link is emailed to you and is valid for 1 hour.
