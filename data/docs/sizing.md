# Sizing & Fit Guide

## How to measure your foot
Stand on a sheet of paper with your heel against a wall. Mark the tip of your longest toe and measure from the wall to the mark. Measure in the evening, when feet are slightly swollen, and measure both feet. Use the larger measurement.

## Size chart (US Men's)
| US Men's size | Foot length (cm) | Foot length (in) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 25.0 | 9.8 |
| 8 | 25.9 | 10.2 |
| 9 | 26.7 | 10.5 |
| 10 | 27.5 | 10.8 |
| 11 | 28.4 | 11.2 |
| 12 | 29.3 | 11.5 |

Women's sizes: add 1.5 to the Men's size to get the Women's size (Men's 8 equals Women's 9.5). If your foot measurement falls between two sizes, choose the larger size.

## Fit by style
- AeroRun (AeroRun 1 and AeroRun 2): runs about half a size small. We recommend ordering half a size up.
- CourtLine Classic: true to size.
- TrailBlazer GTX: true to size with a roomier toe box, which suits thicker hiking socks.

## Width options
All styles come in standard width (D). Wide width (2E) is available only in CourtLine Classic and TrailBlazer GTX, in Men's sizes 8-13. AeroRun is not available in wide.

## Available sizes
Men's US 6-13, with half sizes available through size 12. Not every color is available in every size. Sizes that are sold out can be joined with the Notify Me button on the product page.

## Fit guarantee
If your shoes don't fit, your first size exchange per order is free within the return window. See the Returns Policy for how to start an exchange. Shoes must be unworn to qualify, so try them on indoors first.
