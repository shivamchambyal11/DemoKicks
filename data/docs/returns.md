# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days start on the day the carrier marks your order as delivered, not the day you placed it.

Holiday extension: orders delivered between November 1 and December 24 can be returned until January 31 of the following year.

## Eligibility
Shoes must be unworn, in original condition, and returned in the original shoebox with all tags and accessories (extra laces, insoles). Trying shoes on indoors, on a clean carpeted surface, is fine. Returns showing signs of wear, outdoor dirt, creasing from walking, odor, or a missing shoebox are rejected and shipped back to you at your cost.

## Non-returnable items
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout (clearance and last-season styles)
- Gift cards
- Socks, and laces or insoles sold separately (hygiene reasons)
- Personalized or custom-designed items

Final Sale items that arrive with a manufacturing defect are still covered by our Warranty Policy.

## How to start a return
1. Go to demokicks.example/returns and enter your order number and the email used at checkout.
2. Select the items and the reason for return. You will receive an RMA number and a return label by email.
3. Place the shoebox inside a shipping bag or outer box. Do not tape or write on the shoebox itself.
4. Drop the package at the carrier within 14 days, because the label expires after that.

## Return shipping cost
For US orders, returns of a defective, damaged, or wrong item use a free prepaid label. For any other reason, a flat $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund. Size exchanges are free. International orders do not get prepaid labels (see the Shipping Policy).

## Refunds
After your return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method 5-7 business days after approval, and you will get a confirmation email. Your bank may need extra time to show the credit.

Shipping fees paid at checkout are refunded only when the return is due to our error (defective, damaged, or wrong item). If you prefer store credit, it is issued within 1 business day of approval.

## Exchanges
Each order gets one free size or color exchange within the return window. We ship the replacement pair as soon as the carrier scans your return. If the size you need is out of stock, you can choose a refund instead.

## Missing returns
If your return shows no delivery scan 21 days after the label was created, contact support@demokicks.example with your tracking number and we will open a carrier trace.
